IOv2::tz_level::none
@ none
Carries no time-zone field; z and Z are both matched literally.
IOv2::tz_level::offset
@ offset
Parses the UTC offset from z; Z is still matched literally.
IOv2::tz_level::zone
@ zone
Everything in offset, plus Z parsed into a zone identity and abbreviation.
