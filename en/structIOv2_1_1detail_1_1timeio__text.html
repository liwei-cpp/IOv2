Detailed Description

template<typename CharT>
struct IOv2::detail::timeio_text< CharT >

A locale's names and formats as timeio_conf<CharT> keeps them, the formats completed and normalized. Built by timeio_c_text or timeio_text_from, for the narrow and the wide configurations alike.
