Detailed Description

Constrains type T to provide all mandatory interfaces required by a converter:

  • Device management: device(), detach(), attach()
  • Lifecycle control: bos() (establish initial IO state), main_cont_beg() (transition into the main content phase)
  • Behavior and status interaction: adjust() (apply a behavior policy), retrieve() (extract internal state)
