Detailed Description

Windows (UCRT) backend of the monetary facet: localeconv(), read under the thread locale a clocale_user switched to.

  • The UCRT's lconv has none of C99's international format flags (int_p_cs_precedes and the rest); the national flags stand in for them, as in libc++ on Windows, while the international fractional digits still come from int_frac_digits. en-US's international format is therefore USD1,234.56.
  • The flags are the ones the UCRT works out from NLS's ICURRENCY/INEGCURR, as the MSVC STL and libc++ take them. That mapping loses something: INEGCURR 12 ($ -1.1) and 13 (1.1- $) are a sep_by_space of 2 in C's terms, and the UCRT gives 1, so nl-NL's negative amounts come out as €- 1.234,56; sep_by_space is never 2 here.
  • int_curr_symbol comes from NLS's LOCALE_SINTLSYMBOL and is the three letters alone ("USD"), without the fourth character C describes.
  • The strings also come in _W_ wide forms, which the wide configurations use as they are; the narrow fields are in the locale's code page, which approximates what it cannot hold (French_France.1252's narrow thousands separator is 0xA0).
