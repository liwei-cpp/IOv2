Detailed Description

template<typename TByte>
requires (std::is_same_v<TByte, char8_t> || std::is_same_v<TByte, char>)
class IOv2::detail::utf8_utf16_codec< TByte >

Conversion between UTF-8 bytes and 16-bit wchar_t (UTF-16), with half a surrogate pair held across calls; instantiated only where wchar_t_is_utf16 holds. Shared by two kernels: codecvt_kernel<char8_t, wchar_t> (TByte is char8_t), and on Windows codecvt_kernel<char, wchar_t> under a UTF-8 locale (TByte is char). Pointer ranges are not checked; the kernels do that.

A character outside the BMP takes two wchar_ts internally (a surrogate pair), which a call boundary can split, so half of one is held:

  • writing: a run that ends in a high surrogate has it held, writing no bytes, and encoded with the low surrogate that opens the next run;
  • reading: a character outside the BMP decoded with one place left in the output has its high surrogate written and its low one held; the next in_helper hands that over first, consuming no bytes.

Decode UTF-8 bytes into UTF-16 units. A low surrogate held from the previous call comes first, consuming no bytes; a character outside the BMP met with one place left in the output has its high surrogate written and its low one held. An incomplete character at the end and an invalid sequence are left unconsumed (from stops at its start).

Returns
false at an invalid UTF-8 sequence, true otherwise. How many units were written is how far to advanced.

Encode a run of UTF-16 units into UTF-8, as many as fit. Each character is started only with at least 4 bytes left. A run that ends in a high surrogate has it held (from moves past it, no bytes written).

Returns
false at a unit that cannot be encoded: a lone low surrogate, or a high surrogate not followed by a low one (from is left on the high surrogate; if that was held from the previous run, on this run's first unit, and the held one is dropped). true otherwise, including when encoding stopped for lack of room.
